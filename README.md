### 🔬 About Me

I work to bring robots out of the lab and make them capable of performing manipulation and navigation tasks robustly and intelligently — robots that don't hesitate to interact with the real world. My research bridges **classical control and motion planning** with **modern learning-based methods**.

- 🎓 Ph.D. in Automatic Control, Robotics &amp; Computer Vision — Institute of Industrial and Control Engineering (IOC), Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), Barcelona, Spain
- 📜 Thesis: *Physics-based Motion Planning for Grasping and Manipulation* — knowledge-oriented physics-based planning among movable obstacles, grasping in clutter, and planning with temporal goals
- 🛰️ Former Project Manager for the **MBZIRC Maritime Grand Challenge** (heterogeneous USV + UAV + manipulator systems in GNSS-denied conditions)
- 🏢 Previously R&D Engineer at **ITK System Engineering (BOSCH Group)** — medical robotics, AR, and software testing
- 🌍 Visiting scholar at the **Kavraki Lab (Rice University)** and the **Institute of Robotics and Mechatronics (DLR, German Aerospace Center)**

---

### 🎯 Current Focus

- **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models** for robot manipulation
- **LLM / ontology-driven task and motion planning** — refining prompts with knowledge-based reasoning for context-aware symbolic plans
- **Autonomous marine navigation** — environmental monitoring, maritime security, and port inspection
- **Robotic perception** — visual servoing, tracking, and underwater vision
